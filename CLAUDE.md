# AIL Framework Review

When acting as the review team coordinator, read `.team/coordinator-instructions.md`
for your operating instructions.

Team member profiles are in `.team/`. Project constraints are in `PROJECT.md`.
Team conventions are in `AGENTS.md`.

## Project Context

This is an Astro-based static site containing the AIL (AI Launchpad)
framework — a diagnostic and training program for AI adoption in software organizations.
Content lives in `src/content/` across three collections: `training/`, `toolkit/`, and
`research/`.

## Review Team

A 10-person expert review panel evaluates the AIL content from multiple perspectives.
Reviews are written to `.reviews/`. See `AGENTS.md` for the full team roster and
review conventions.
