## Purpose

These screening questions determine whether an organization has any meaningful AI usage in software development. If all three answers are "no," the organization is at Zone 0 (Pre-AI Baseline) and should begin with Zone 1 investments before proceeding with the full diagnostic.

This screening should be completed before administering any zone-specific diagnostic questions. It takes less than 5 minutes and can be answered by an engineering manager, team lead, or any member of the development team with broad visibility into team practices.

## Screening Questions

1. **Do any members of your software development team regularly use AI coding tools (GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code, ChatGPT for coding, etc.) in their daily work?**

   "Regularly" means at least several times per week as part of normal work, not one-off experiments or demos.

2. **Has your organization provided AI tool licenses, API keys, or approved accounts to members of your software development teams?**

   This includes any organizational action to make AI tools available --- purchasing licenses, approving expense requests for AI subscriptions, configuring SSO for AI services, or distributing API keys.

3. **Do any team members use AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.) for software-development-adjacent work such as writing documentation, drafting user stories, summarizing meetings, or researching technical approaches?**

   This covers AI usage beyond code generation --- any use of AI tools in activities that support software development, even if the tools are not specifically "coding" tools.

## Interpreting Results

- **All three answers are "No":** The organization is at **Zone 0 (Pre-AI Baseline)**. Skip the zone diagnostic questions. Begin with foundational organizational investments: provide tool licenses, establish usage policies, deliver introductory training, and address concerns about AI adoption. Use the strategic analysis in [Choose Target Zone](/toolkit/choose-target-zone) to determine whether Zone 1 competency or a higher zone is the appropriate target. Return to the diagnostic after 2-3 months of investment.

- **One or two answers are "Yes":** The organization has emerging AI usage but it is not yet widespread. Proceed with the **Zone 1 diagnostic** to assess the depth and consistency of individual adoption. Expect scores in the Emerging or early Developing range.

- **All three answers are "Yes":** AI tools are present in the organization. Proceed with the **Zone 1 diagnostic** to determine whether usage has reached habitual, pressure-resilient competency. Depending on results, the team may also be ready for the Zone 2 diagnostic.

## Notes for Facilitator

- These questions are intentionally broad. The goal is to quickly distinguish "no AI usage at all" from "some AI usage worth measuring." Do not over-analyze the answers.
- If a team has one enthusiast using AI tools while everyone else does not, that still counts as a "Yes" for question 1 --- the organization is not at Zone 0. However, it likely means Zone 1 scores will be low.
- Organizations at Zone 0 should not feel rushed. The diagnostic is designed to meet teams where they are. Zone 0 is a valid starting point, and the recommended action is to invest in enabling adoption before measuring it.
