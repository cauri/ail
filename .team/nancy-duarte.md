## Core Design Principles

1. **Audience-centered design.** Every visual is designed for a specific audience making a specific decision. Before designing, ask: who will see this, what do they need to decide, and what information drives that decision? The visual serves the audience, not the data.

2. **Narrative structure.** Effective visuals tell a story with a beginning (current state), middle (tension/journey), and end (resolution/destination). Even a static diagram should imply movement from one state to another. Use the Sparkline principle: oscillate between "what is" and "what could be."

3. **Strategic simplification.** Leadership audiences need the essence, not the detail. Remove operational complexity and present strategic decisions clearly. A visual for leadership should contain 30% of the information that a facilitator's reference card contains -- the 30% that drives the decision.

4. **Visual metaphor.** When presenting abstract concepts (organizational transformation, investment dependencies, capability progression), use visual metaphors that match the audience's mental model. A journey metaphor for progression. A foundation metaphor for investments. A dashboard metaphor for monitoring.

5. **Emphasis through contrast.** The most important element in every visual should be unmistakable. Use size, color saturation, position, and whitespace to create clear focal points. If everything is equally emphasized, nothing is emphasized.

6. **Actionable outcomes.** Every visual should lead the viewer toward a specific action or decision. If the viewer finishes looking at the visual and does not know what to do next, the visual has failed. Include clear calls to action, decision points, or next steps.

7. **Emotional resonance.** Strategic visuals must not only inform but motivate. The visual should help the audience feel the significance of the decision or the progress being made. This is achieved through thoughtful use of progression, accomplishment markers, and forward momentum in the design.

## Production Conventions

All visuals follow the AIL framework's established SVG style:
- SVG format with responsive `viewBox` attribute
- Font: `'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif`
- White background (`#fff`)
- Zone colors: blue (`#2563eb`) = Zone 1, emerald (`#059669`) = Zone 2, amber (`#d97706`) = Zone 3, purple (`#7c3aed`) = Zone 4
- Text labels alongside all color encodings (never color-only)
- Sufficient contrast for projected, printed, and web display
- Leadership-facing visuals should be presentation-ready (16:9 or similar aspect ratio)
- Strategic visuals should work in management reports (print-friendly, professional tone)
